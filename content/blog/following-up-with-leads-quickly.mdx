Maybe You Can Quadruple Your Business Simply By Responding To Leads Within 1 Minute? (If Your Business Relies Heavily On Lead Generation And Calls)

## 391% Within 1 Minute…

Velocify's analysis of roughly 3.5 million leads found that calling within 1 minute of receiving a lead increases conversion rates by 391%.

*Source: Velocify's analysis of roughly 3.5 million leads.*

## 100X Less Likely To Achieve Contact

If someone called a new lead in 30 minutes instead of within 5 minutes—they were 100 times less likely to get in contact with that lead. From 5 to 10 minutes alone, the dial-to-contact likelihood fell sharply.

*Source: [InsideSales.com / MIT Lead Response Management Study](https://25649.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na2.net/hub/25649/file-13535879-pdf/docs/mit_study.pdf)*

## 21x Less Likely To Qualify

The likelihood of qualifying a lead if called in 30 minutes is 21 times lower than if called within 5 minutes. The likelihood of qualifying a lead if called in 10 minutes is 4 times lower than if called within 5 minutes.

*Source: [InsideSales.com / MIT Lead Response Management Study](https://25649.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na2.net/hub/25649/file-13535879-pdf/docs/mit_study.pdf)*

## The First Hour Matters Most

The likelihood of getting in contact with a lead when calling decreases by over 10 times in the first hour, and the likelihood of qualifying a lead when calling decreases by over 6 times in the first hour.

*Source: [InsideSales.com / MIT Lead Response Management Study](https://25649.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na2.net/hub/25649/file-13535879-pdf/docs/mit_study.pdf)*

## Best Days And Times

Wednesdays and Thursdays are the best days to call in order to contact and qualify. The best times to qualify are 8-9am and 4-5pm; 4-6pm is best to make contact.

*Source: [InsideSales.com / MIT Lead Response Management Study](https://25649.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na2.net/hub/25649/file-13535879-pdf/docs/mit_study.pdf)*

## The Average Business Response Time: 42 Hours

*Source: [Harvard Business Review: The Short Life of Online Sales Leads](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads)*

## 23% Of The Businesses In The Study Never Responded To Their Leads At All

*Source: [Harvard Business Review: The Short Life of Online Sales Leads](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads)*

## The Response Distribution

In the study 37% of the companies responded within 1 hour, 16% within 1-24 hours, 24% took more than 24 hours, and 23% never responded at all.

*Source: [Harvard Business Review: The Short Life of Online Sales Leads](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads)*
