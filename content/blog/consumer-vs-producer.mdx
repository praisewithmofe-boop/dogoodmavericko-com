## Consumerism Is Obviously A Massive Problem In Today's Society

But The Opposite Of Being A Consumer Is NOT To Just Be A Good Steward, Save, Budget And Live Below Your Means.

A Careful Consumer Is Still Primarily Consuming. The Real Opposite Of A Consumer Is Being A Producer.

## The Opposite Of Consumerism Is Producerism!

Yes I Think You Should Absolutely Be A Good Steward, Stick To A Budget And Live Below Your Means! But I Think It Should Be Done With A Focus On Becoming A World Class PRODUCER!

You Can Only Save So Much, There Is A Very Real “Glass Ceiling” To How Much You Can Save—You Can Not Save More Money Than You Have And What You Earn/Get. What You Can Produce And As A Result Earn On The Other Hand Has A Lot Less Limits!

For a lot of people that might mean entrepreneurship—but it does NOT have to be risky things like starting a revolutionary Start-Up, there are a lot of options these days of things that can be done with relatively low risk—even while still working a full-time job as an employee.

**Becoming A World Class PRODUCER Has A WAY Higher Chance Of Massively Changing Your Life Than Becoming A World Class Budgeter And Saver!**
