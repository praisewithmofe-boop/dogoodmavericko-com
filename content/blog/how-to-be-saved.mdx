> “⁹If you declare with your mouth, ‘Jesus is Lord,’ and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved. ¹⁰For it is with your heart that you believe and are justified, and it is with your mouth that you profess your faith and are saved.”
>
> — Romans 10:9-10 NIV

## That's How Easy The Gospel Is!

Repent from your sins, turn away from your sins, and put your belief and trust in Jesus Christ and you will be saved and get eternal life.

None of us can achieve salvation through works, by grace we are saved through faith in Jesus Christ, it is a gift from God. But it is up to us to receive it—through faith.

> “⁸For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: It is the gift of God: ⁹Not of works, lest any man should boast.”
>
> — Ephesians 2:8-9 KJV

**In Case You Who Are Reading This Are Not Already Saved; If You Are Not Already A Christian, If You Want To Be Saved You Can Simply Pray This:**

---

## Prayer For Salvation

Lord, it says in Romans 3:23 that: *“For all have sinned and come short of the glory of God;”* and that includes me, I am a sinner, and I can not save myself—I need a savior; I need you Jesus, in Acts 4:12 it says about You: *“Neither is there salvation in any other: for there is none other name under heaven given among men, whereby we must be saved.”*

And in John 3:16 it says: *“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”*

I am now choosing to make you: Jesus Christ my Lord and Savior, I put my belief and trust in You. Jesus Christ I believe you died on the cross for my sins and that you rose again the third day.

I invite you into my life, into my heart, I am now choosing to make You, Jesus Christ my Lord and Savior, I repent and turn away from my sins. I put my belief and trust in You Jesus so that I will be saved and have everlasting life, I pray all of this in the Name of Jesus Christ, Amen.

---

## Congratulations!

If you were not already saved, and you sincerely prayed this prayer, congratulations!! I highly advise you to tell someone, find a solid Church, read your Bible and pray every day, and also get baptized.
