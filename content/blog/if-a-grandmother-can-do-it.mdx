A few months ago, I interviewed a grandmother for my online education and community platform: Terrific 30

I interviewed her about her Success Story of how she built her online Paid Community…

It was super inspiring stuff!

## From $0 To $10K Profit In 90 Days…

She basically told the story of how she started from $0 and an almost non-existent following on social media.

And she mainly focused on going live on YouTube and on posting on TikTok daily.

In 90 days she reached about $14,000 per month in revenue (probably well over $10K profit)

## 2 Hours Of Daily Moneymaking Activities

In the interview she also shared the concept she has where she has 2 hours per day of money making activities—after those 2 hours she has done her main moneymaking activities for the day.

Some days she works more, some days not.

And after her first 90 days she reached about $10K in profit per month—since then she has gone on to make millions of dollars.
